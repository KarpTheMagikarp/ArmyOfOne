    ArmyOfOne is a fan-made tool that makes setting up Final Fantasy XIII, XIII-2, and Lightning Returns on PC easy for new or returning players, without dragging files around by hand. Pick a game and hit Apply for the essential fixes, then check out Mods and Settings for HD packs, extra mods, and graphics options. Anything it installs can (hopefully) be taken back off, so feel free to experiment. Enjoy!
